01 / Perspective

Different places.
Different ways to see things.

Buenos Aires, Paris and Barcelona have each brought new people, working styles and perspectives into my life. Moving between them taught me to adapt without losing my curiosity.

02 / Growth

Always room
to figure out more.

New tools, new subjects, new challenges. Learning is part of the work, not something that happens only outside it.

03 / People

Good work is
rarely solo work.

Different strengths and honest conversations often lead to better ideas — and make the journey more interesting.

04 / Resilience

Change happens.
Keep your footing.

Starting over in new places and stepping into unfamiliar situations have taught me to stay resourceful, make decisions with the information available and keep moving when plans change.

A bit of
everything.

Different roles, different industries, one recurring theme: connecting the people, details and decisions that help things move forward.

01 / Commercial

From first conversation
to long-term relationship.

B2B and B2C sales, prospecting, discovery, demos, negotiation, closing, account growth, renewals and partnerships. The job is as much about understanding people as it is about the numbers.

02 / Operations

The moving parts.

Daily operations, onboarding, incidents, events, budgets, logistics, coordination and process improvements — often all in the same day.

03 / Decisions

People + data
+ a bit of judgment.

Client needs, CRM pipelines, KPIs, conversion, churn risks and commercial opportunities. Knowing what to look at matters; knowing what to do with it matters more.

04 / The unexpected

Plans change.
The work still needs doing.

Last-minute changes, tricky client situations and competing priorities have been part of the picture — from running day-to-day operations to coordinating international events. Staying calm, reordering priorities and finding a workable solution is part of the job.
